# ChemLab — river release art brief

Mixed game illustration/motion; mobile web, Android and iOS; 2026-10-08. Owner: Євгеній. Production: Codex, built-in image generation and game renderer. No external artist commissions or paid asset purchases.

## 1. Context
Replace the rejected flat canal without moving saved buildings. Acceptance: no angular widening, repeating square water patches, toy bridge or assets floating over land; test actual phone-scale rendering before publication.
## 2. Concept
A shallow, living woodland river with visible depth and grounded construction. Reject neon cyan water, straight concrete edging and decorative buildings inside buildings.
## 3. Audience
ChemLab players on phones. The town should feel inhabitable and coherent, not assembled from incompatible stickers.
## 4. Subjects
Weathered timber bridge, stone abutments, rounded wet rocks, soil banks and sparse reeds. No characters in the new atlas; existing residents remain. No logos, text, military subjects or copied reference composition.
## 5. Composition
Four isolated transparent assets in a 2×2 atlas: bridge; mossy rock cluster; damp pebble/silt edge; reeds. Orthographic isometric camera; bridge axis upper-left to lower-right, river axis upper-right to lower-left. Gameplay coordinates remain authoritative. Mobile checks at 375/430 px and 85–240% camera zoom; desktop at 1440 px.
## 6. Lighting
Soft daylight from upper left, warm wood, neutral stone, cool contact shadows. Ground-contact shadows must follow footprint and not become black halos.
## 7. Color and treatment
Natural brown wood, subdued moss, desaturated blue-green water; warm shallows and dark middle. Fine surface detail without white wave noise covering the entire river.
## 8. Tone
Grounded, tactile, calm, readable. Continuous downstream flow; small eddies near rocks; reduced-motion mode static. No new sound in this patch.
## 9. Reference
User's supplied river/log scene is the visual target; rejected IMG_9760 is the negative example. Take material realism, proportion, bank relief and consistent lighting, not its characters or composition. Existing ChemLab buildings remain the compatibility reference. A full external moodboard is deferred; no external imagery is copied into the game.
## 10. Avoid
Rectangular water clip masks, ruler-straight banks, hard elbow widening, baked-in water inside the bridge sprite, oversaturated grass/water contrast, undocumented performance claims.
## 11. Deliverables and usage
Versioned transparent PNG atlas, native canvas renderer module, geography regression tests, release checklist. Original generated assets; user references guide visual qualities only. Prompt is recorded in this file's companion asset note. No third-party purchased asset rights are assumed.
## 12. Timeline
Brief → generate/inspect → integrate → actual-size screenshots → test → release candidate. No store-submission date is promised without signing and device QA.
## 13. Approvals
Codex verifies technical integration; Євгеній approves visual direction. Iterate against visible result; passing unit tests alone is insufficient.
## 14. Talent
No commissioned talent. Built-in image generation supplies raster materials; code supplies geometry and animation.
## 15. Open questions / sign-off
Store identity, package ID and signing remain owner decisions. This brief implements the user's requested revision; final visual approval is not presumed.
