# v42 — coherent architectural family and fields

Replaced houses, sawmills, factories, research laboratory, ranches, schools and granaries with one shared camera/material family. Four house stages; two industry/ranch/school/granary stages. Logical upgrade levels and saves are unchanged. Other specialist buildings still use existing art.

Fields now use a separate four-stage crop atlas with no fences or raised platform. Field raster projection is normalized to a .64 height/width ratio to include stalk height above the .56 ground plane. Ranches use two small animated sheep rather than baked livestock. Door anchors adjusted for the new front-left facades; per-level fine calibration remains.

Built-in imagegen used for both assets. Alpha verified at empty pixels; formats converted to WebP, no opaque background flattening.
