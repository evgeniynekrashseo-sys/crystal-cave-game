ChemLab — Laboratory Edition

Privacy notice for the city-free laboratory game. Last reviewed: November 8, 2026. This notice describes the current build without an advertising SDK. It must be updated before any ads or analytics launch.

English

No account is needed. Puzzle progress, earned virtual rewards, preferences and achievements are saved locally on your device. We do not currently operate an account server, analytics SDK, advertising SDK, or in-app payments in this edition. We do not automatically receive your puzzle progress.

Our web demo is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub, as the hosting provider, processes technical connection data such as your IP address under GitHub's privacy policy. Offline files may be stored in your device's cache.

You can export a backup to a JSON file and import it yourself. Exporting does not transmit it to us. If you share it with someone, they can view the saved game data. To erase your data, delete this site's storage in your browser or uninstall the native app; back up first if you want to retain your progress.

For support, open a GitHub issue. Issues are public; do not share private information. The science content and reactor effects are game simulations, not instructions for real chemical experiments.
