## Changes under review

- Added a new detailed meadow material while retaining the bright village palette and approved building/tube artwork.
- Terrain consumes the complete original image at four image pixels per world pixel; no small crop is enlarged. The procedural meadow remains a fallback during loading or unavailable image data.
- Added a feathered damp-earth transition beside the river and sparse, fixed-position bank grasses/reeds. Only water highlights animate; bank geometry does not use time or parallax.
- Added deterministic fine gravel/earth detail to the existing building plots. Front-corner floor anchors and contact shadows from release 17 remain.
- Updated the complete import/cache graph to release 18 and replaced unused old scene backgrounds in precache with the actual new ground material.
- Existing save keys, progression, chemistry rules and laboratory tube/liquid graphics are preserved.

The river remains decorative world-space scenery; the simulation's original water-cell/pathfinding model was not migrated in this visual fix.

## Image asset and prompt provenance

**Project asset:** `dist/settlement-meadow-v18.png`  
**Actual measured output:** 1254×1254 RGB PNG, 3743244 bytes. The prompt requested 2048×2048; the actual output was retained without upscaling.  
**Creation:** Built-in imagegen, one new project-bound ground asset. Existing atlases were not redesigned.

**Final prompt:**

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: a production-ready seamless repeating ground material for ChemLab, a premium mobile isometric village strategy game. Create ONE square 2048x2048 texture of a sunny open meadow, filling every pixel edge to edge. Match richly detailed, almost realistic illustrated 3D cottages, warm stone and timber, teal-blue tiled roofs, and lush trees; this image is ONLY the grass surface below those separate sprites. Orthographic surface view with a constant level of detail everywhere; no horizon or perspective scale falloff. Short golden-green grass, distinct sharply resolved tiny blades with soft olive contact shadows, small clover leaves, discreet fine straw and sparse warm earth visible between blades. Natural irregular density with soft small variations; retain light yellow-green palette and calm open spaces so buildings remain readable. Fine tactile micro-detail and soft sun from the upper left, no painted blurry blobs or noise filter. Truly seamless matching left/right and top/bottom boundaries; no grid, seams, border, vignette, large directional shadow, or central focal object. No houses, roads, rivers, people, trees, large flowers, icons, text, UI, watermarks or photographic depth of field. This is a usable game terrain tile, not a screenshot or a mockup.
