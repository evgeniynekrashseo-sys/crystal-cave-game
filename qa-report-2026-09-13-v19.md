# QA Report: ChemLab — camera limits and city panel scrolling, release 19

**Date:** 2026-09-13

**Scope:** Focused regression review of settlement controls. This is not a complete device or performance audit.

**Authorization:** User requested continued fixes after publication; publication authorization remains in effect.

## Reproduced defects

| Defect | Evidence before the fix | Result of the fix |
|---|---|---|
| Catalog jumps after choosing a building | Actual release-18 Chrome UI: horizontal scroll 1494 → 1 after choosing Road | Retain catalog offset when choosing a tool or a plot |
| Camera sticks after reversing at a limit | Camera controller: drag 200 pixels into a 50-pixel pan bound; reversing 10 pixels leaves pan at 50 | Rebase to the actual bounded camera after movement; the same reversal reaches 40 |
| Panel position is lost on refresh/switch | Panel content is rebuilt with `innerHTML` | Keep independent vertical offsets for each panel and the catalog's horizontal offset while the city controller remains alive |

Scroll memory is session-only. It does not add save fields or change the existing settlement save schema.

## Changes

- Pan and pinch use the actual camera as the origin for the next movement, including after reaching a map or zoom limit.
- A drag or pinch continues to suppress plot taps; lifting one finger hands off to pan without a jump.
- City panel scroll positions survive tool selection, plot selection, panel switching, collapse and reopening the city in the same session.
- The complete module and service-worker cache graph is version 19.
- Settlement art assets and laboratory tube/liquid design are unchanged. Version 18 meadow and river-bank detail remain in use.
